# YES introduction media

`yes-intro.png` and `yes-intro.mp4` are composed screenshots/video of the YES
introduction, made from the [source example](../examples/yes-intro/README.md).
They are demonstration works, not a character asset pack.

- Standing artwork: 坂本アヒル.
- Characters: 東北ずん子・ずんだもんプロジェクト.

- [Character guidelines](https://zunko.jp/guideline.html).
